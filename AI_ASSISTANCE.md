# AI assistance disclosure

The initial implementation and documentation of ckanext-actor-registry were
developed by Björn Hagström with assistance from OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex.

AI tools assisted with research, design discussion, code drafting, refactoring,
documentation and operational verification. Product decisions, deployment
authorization and acceptance of the resulting work remain human decisions.

The human maintainer is the author, copyright holder and accountable project
maintainer. AI systems are not listed as authors or maintainers. Before a public
release, the maintainer is expected to review the released diff, verify the
documented behavior and accept responsibility for correctness, security,
licensing and provenance.

Contributors should disclose material generative-AI assistance in their pull
request description. Disclosure does not replace review, tests or the
contributor's responsibility for the submitted material.
